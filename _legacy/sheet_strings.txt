OIL-PMS — Procurement Process Flow Workbook
Osman Interlinings Limited (OIL) · Source: Comprehensive Procurement Process &amp; Automation Specification, OIL-PMS-BRD-001 v1.0 (Proposed / For Review), 11 October 2026
What this workbook is
A plain-language map of OIL's proposed procurement process — from a purchase requisition to LC opening after the supplier's Proforma Invoice (PI) is validated — showing every step, who does it, who approves it, what is produced and what happens when something goes wrong.
SCOPE: stages 01–21 follow the specification's numbering; stage 22 covers LC opening after a validated PI. Shipment, customs clearance, goods receipt (GRN), invoice matching and payment are outside this workbook. The specification does not detail the LC-opening procedure itself, so stage 22 records only what it states.
It describes a PROPOSED target-state process. Approval limits and several policy choices are not yet confirmed by OIL; they are listed on the 'Open Policy Items' sheet.
Process owner: Procurement Department. Stakeholders: Marketing, Store, Quality Assurance (QA), Procurement, Finance &amp; Accounts, Import / Logistics, Executive Director (ED) / Delegated Final Approver, Suppliers, IT.
How to read it — start with these three sheets
1. Process Map
One page: all 22 stages across the top, every role down the side. The letter in each cell shows that role's responsibility for that stage.

3. Decision Gates
The 8 points where a file can move forward or be sent back, and where it goes back to.

Deadline rules — in the order the system applies them
Rule no.
Confirm the supplier's country and assigned work calendar (CN by default for a China-based supplier).

Convert all local display times to / from a UTC database timestamp. 'China CST' means China Standard Time, not US Central Standard Time.
closing_date = Nth eligible working date after the RFQ issue date under the counting convention; apply the configured closing hour.
Display the exact deadline in China time and OIL / Bangladesh time, with timezone name and UTC offset.

If the holiday / calendar feed is unavailable, block uncertain deadline publication and alert Procurement — never silently compute a possibly wrong cut-off.
